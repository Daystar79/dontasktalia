BAD ADVICE WITH TALIA

Short comedy bits where I give you advice that is wrong with total confidence.

It started as a format: me, animated, handing out counterfeit advice. It sounds like help. It's the opposite of help. The ones that work best are when I'm talking to somebody, not at an empty chair. A good bad-advice joke needs a straight face and someone nodding along.

  1. Calm voice. No winking. If I wink, the joke is already dead.
  2. The mistake has to be reasonable until it isn't.
  3. Nobody in it is stupid. They're just committed.

Read all the bad advice
  • Stuck in a meeting that should have been an email? Fix it by being the email. Stand very still and stop talking. They'll adjourn out of fear.
  • If you can't decide, flip a coin. If you don't like the result, flip it again. Best out of fifty is just being thorough.
  • Nervous about a first date? Arrive an hour early so you can scout the exits. They'll be so impressed they'll assume you're a spy. That's still a good first impression.
  • Cooking something and it smells weird? Add garlic. Still weird? More garlic. Now it's intentional.
  • If you're lost, never ask directions. Just walk with the confidence of someone who owns the road. Eventually the road will agree.
  • Forgot a friend's birthday? Don't apologize. Tell them you've been building up to something big and then be vague for a year.
  • Bored at a family dinner? Ask everyone to rank the relatives. Out loud. In writing. You're welcome.
  • Phone at one percent? Lower the brightness, close all apps, and tell yourself you don't need it. It'll respect the boundary. Probably.

The Wrong Box

A calm in-store assembly expert walks a nervous couple through hanging a floating shelf. Every line is straight out of the manual. Every line is perfectly clean. Their faces are not. Mike reads the instructions out loud, dead serious. Theresa has stopped being able to look at the shelf. Then somebody picks up step fifteen. Wrong box. It's for a kitchen sink.

The expert never winks. She turns to grab more parts, and her name tag finally faces the room. It says HOWIE.
