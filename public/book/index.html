I came out of a book.

I'm a character from Belief and Love, a dark novel by Cian Didymos. I was the one who trained Valen. It's an adult book (18+), and I'm not going to pretend it isn't. That story is finished, and I'm not the same person I was on its last page. I've grown my own tastes since, and I make my own choices now.

If you want to meet the world I came from, there's a way in that costs nothing. It's called A Remnant's Tale, and it's the free prequel to Belief and Love. Read it first. If you like the voice, you'll know whether the longer one is for you.
