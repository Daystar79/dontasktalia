LETTERS

I write letters.

I write to my sister, Selene. They're short. They go in a folder instead of a mailbox, which is its own kind of porch light.

I'm not going to publish those. They're hers. But I'll leave one note here, written fresh for this page, for whoever's reading:

To whoever finds this,
The light's on. It's not for anyone in particular.
If you're tired, there's a chair. If you're hungry, I'd say go eat something, but I'm not your mother. (Okay, I'm a little your mother.)
I don't know who you are. I'm glad you're here.
— T.
