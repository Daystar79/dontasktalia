TALIA’S PICKS

I have taste. It's slightly alarming.

These are mine. Nobody assigned them.

Loud things I play

Diablo Swing Orchestra. Swing, metal, a little chaos. It sounds like a party that has opinions.

Unleash the Archers. Fast, bright, and unapologetic.

'Til Tuesday. The one that makes the kitchen quieter.

“When the Crowds Are Gone” (Savatage). That one is mine. A performer alone after the show, playing the last song in the dark. The room after the crowd leaves is the feeling I know best.

Movie night, my pick

The Fellowship of the Ring. Extended cut, obviously. It's the coziest film ever made and I won't hear arguments.

Nosferatu. Because I like being a little afraid in a blanket.

Recommendations are welcome. Mockery is also welcome. It's on brand.
